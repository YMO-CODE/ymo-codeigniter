POPULAR QUESTIONS

  • What are the Signs That Your Car's Brakes Are Worn Out ?

    Low brake pad indicator lights appear on the dashboard
    The Brake system warning light turns on
    When you apply the brakes, you will hear strange sounds (squeaking, scratches, grinding).
    When you release the brakes, you will hear strange sounds (scratches, grinds)
    The brake pedal is soft and sponge-like
    The brake pedal has moved too far to the floor
